Swap · Stage 1 · Kochi first

Establishing Swap in its first city

Don't Buy. Don't Sell. Just Exchange.

A six-month plan to take Swap from waitlist to a working exchange community in Kochi, from November 2026 to April 2027. It has growth ideas, an editable forecast, a phase-by-phase plan with go/no-go gates, and the risks to watch.

All figures are planning assumptions, not results. Change the inputs in the forecast and every number on this page updates.

What Stage 1 must prove

Three things, in order

1 · People will list

Members post real items they own. Target: 30% of new members list an item in their first week.

2 · Swaps actually happen

Matches turn into completed exchanges. Target: completed swaps reach 4% or more of monthly active users.

3 · Someone will pay

Local businesses buy ads and members upgrade to Pro. Target: first ₹1 lakh in monthly revenue.

Growth ideas

How we fill the shelves and the feed

A new marketplace lives or dies on supply. Every idea below either brings items in, turns matches into swaps, or makes Swap visible in Kochi.

Supply · Nov–Dec

Swap Drives at campuses and IT parks

Drop-off desks at 2 colleges and Infopark/SmartCity. The team photographs and lists items on members' behalf, so 1,500 listings are live before public launch.

Execution

Four phases, each with a gate

Don't move to the next phase until its gate is met. If a gate is missed, fix the weak step before spending more on growth.

Weeks 1–6 · Nov to mid-Dec0 · FoundationGet ready to open
  • Register the HPY entity for Swap and file the trademark. Search “Swap” in classes 9, 35 and 42 early, because it's a common word.
  • Publish the Terms, Privacy Policy (DPDP Act), Community Guidelines and Grievance Officer details.
  • Build the MVP: sign-up with OTP, swap posts, Have/Want matching, chat, profiles, reporting.
  • Open the waitlist and recruit 20 campus ambassadors.
  • Shortlist 100 local businesses for Founding Advertisers.
Gate: legal pages live · MVP passes testing on budget Android phones · 2,000 on the waitlist
Weeks 7–10 · mid-Dec to Jan1 · SeedClosed beta
  • Invite 500 beta members from 2 campuses and Kakkanad IT parks.
  • Run the Swap Drives to reach 1,500 listings.
  • Hold the first Fort Kochi Sunday Swap Meet.
  • Fix the top 10 points where people drop off in the swap flow.
Gate: 30% of beta members list an item · 100 completed swaps · average rating 4.3 or higher
Weeks 11–16 · Jan to Feb2 · Launch KochiPublic launch
  • Hold a public launch event, plus a press and creator day with Kochi creators.
  • Turn on Events, Groups and Stories, with ambassadors running the campus groups.
  • Open the web Ads Manager and sign the first 10 Founding Advertisers.
  • Open Pro and Pro Plus plans (bidding goes live once auctions launch).
Gate: 6,500 monthly active users · swap rate of 4% or more · 20% of members still active after 30 days · 10 paying advertisers
Weeks 17–26 · Mar to Apr3 · Grow & earnMonetise and expand
  • Sign an RBI-authorised payment partner, turn on SwapSafe escrow, and launch Pro auctions with item verification.
  • Start Groups in Thrissur, Thiruvananthapuram and Kozhikode, led by ambassadors.
  • Run the Textbook Swap Fair and Vishu Swap Week, and the first Live Auction Night.
  • Open self-serve ads to all businesses.
Gate to Stage 2: 20,000 monthly active users · ₹1 lakh+ monthly revenue · fewer than 1% of completed swaps disputed
